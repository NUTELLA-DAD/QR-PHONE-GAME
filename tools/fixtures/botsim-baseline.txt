# RE-SNAPSHOTTED in S.5h with the forces on (config.FORCES.LIVE: hits and explosions, gusts, rock scrapes, rams and the gunship tether now twist the ship about her centre of mass, and a crowd
# running to the bow tips her, forces.js / balance.js). Compared over 3 seeds x 3 maps and 8 voyagesim runs, hull / kills / wrecks are within noise (see the S.5h notes in SHIP_BUILDING.md).
# The previous numbers were reproduced byte for byte with NO_FORCES=1 (forces off: the engines and sails still twist her, the classic ship has neither) before this re-snapshot.
# Each block is the output of the command on its '$' line, from '--- botsim summary ---' down (the real-time line is dropped).
# Check with: node tools/buildsim.mjs --check-botsim
# RE-SNAPSHOTTED in M.1 (MOVEMENT.md: everything in the sky is stored in world / map coordinates; the camera and the render draw the world once and each ship under her pose).
# The old frame (a ship nailed to the middle of the screen, the world slid past her) and the new one differ in order, not in rules: with the ship held still (throttle pinned to 0, no daring stunts) the two
# play out the SAME game step for step (a 3.4 minute bot run on the open map agrees to a hundredth of a pixel until a float lands on the map edge), but a moving ship meets the sky a little differently
# (shots keep the speed she had when they left her, things that sit beside her are carried by her real speed, the rock is tested where the thing is), so every moving run plays out differently
# (chaos, not a regression). Compared over 20 seeds x 3 maps x 3 min before / after: kills 25.1 -> 23.8, average hull 92.8 -> 91.7 (open 27.9 -> 23.4 kills, route 23.4 -> 23.3, network 24.1 -> 24.6).
# (tools/fixtures/frames and golden.json were re-captured with it.)
# RE-SNAPSHOTTED in M.4 (MOVEMENT.md: forces drive every ship's pose, flight.js). The ship now has a real speed that her engines, sails and the air's drag change by force over her weight, instead of a speed
# ramped straight to the lever. The classic ship's top speed, climb, dive, stop distance, reverse and time to speed are within 10% of before (the capability table of --check-minimum too), but the speed and
# the way she accelerates, brakes and drifts are not the same numbers, so every seeded run plays out differently (chaos, not a regression). Compared over 2 seeds x 3 maps x 4 min before / after: net speed
# 153-201 px/s -> 151-194; voyagesim 10 runs Normal 6/10 victories (golden 4/10), median 39.7 min (37); Easy 5/10, 43.6 min (44.5). (tools/fixtures/frames and golden.json were re-captured with it.)
# RE-SNAPSHOTTED in the difficulty re-tune (config.DIFFICULTY, CREW_SCALE rows 4 and 16, YARD.POWER_SCALE re-tuned with tools/voyagesim.mjs; Normal now takes less damage and fewer shots, so every seeded run plays out differently: chaos, not a regression).
